# What Is Brow Lamination?

If your brows never stay where you put them, brow lamination may be what you have been looking for. It is one of the most popular brow services, and it is easy to understand.

## What brow lamination is

Brow lamination is a semi-permanent treatment that sets your brow hairs in a lifted, brushed-up direction. The result is fuller, fluffier, perfectly groomed-looking brows, with less styling each morning.

It works with the brows you already have. Because it is semi-permanent, it is not a one-day look.

## Who it suits

Lamination can be a good fit if:

- Your brow hairs grow in different directions
- Your brows look sparse or flat and you want more fullness
- You want a polished look that is low effort each day

## Lamination, shaping and tint

Lamination is often combined with other brow services:

- **Brow Shaping.** Precision shaping and grooming to frame your face.
- **Brow Tint.** Adds color and fullness for natural, longer-lasting brows.

Brow lamination is also part of the **Full Face Rehab** package, which pairs a Dermaplaning Facial, Brow Lamination and a brow wax for a complete refresh.
