# How to Prepare for a Facial and What It Costs

## Getting ready for your facial

A facial works best when your skin arrives ready to be cared for, not layered under makeup or irritation. A few simple steps before your visit help you settle in and make the most of your time in the studio.

### Come with a clean face, or plan a few extra minutes

If you can, arrive with minimal makeup on. It saves time for the treatment itself and lets your esthetician see your skin as it really is. If you're coming straight from work or school, that's fine too. There's always time built in for a proper cleanse before anything else begins.

### Skip strong actives the day before

If you use retinol, exfoliating acids, or any at-home treatment with active ingredients, it's a good idea to pause those the day before your appointment. This gives your skin a calmer starting point and helps it respond well to whatever treatment is planned for you.

### Avoid sun exposure beforehand

Sunburned or freshly tanned skin can be more sensitive to touch and product. If you know a facial is coming up, try to keep sun exposure light in the days before, and always continue sun protection afterward.

### Think about your goals, not just your skin type

Come in with an idea of what you'd like to focus on, whether that's hydration, breakouts, fine lines, or simply a moment to slow down. At Heaven Sent Beauty, every visit starts with a quiet conversation about your skin and your goals before any treatment begins, so nothing is built from a template.

### Give yourself time afterward

Skin can feel a little more reactive right after a facial, especially with exfoliating treatments like dermaplaning. Plan to ease back into makeup and skip anything too vigorous, like an intense workout, for the rest of the day.
