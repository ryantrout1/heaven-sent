# What Is Dermaplaning, and Is It Right for You?

If you have seen "dermaplaning" on a spa menu and wondered what it actually is, you are not alone. It is one of the most asked-about treatments, and it is simpler than it sounds.

## What dermaplaning is

Dermaplaning is a manual exfoliation. A licensed esthetician uses a fine blade, held at a gentle angle, to lift away the dead skin cells sitting on the surface of your face. The same pass removes the soft, fine hair often called peach fuzz.

There are no harsh scrubs and no acids. It is a slow, careful treatment done by hand.

## What it does for your skin

Once that top layer of dull, dead skin is gone, a few things tend to change:

- Skin looks smoother and brighter
- Products can sit more evenly on the skin
- Makeup tends to go on more smoothly

Results are about the surface of the skin, so think of dermaplaning as a refresh, not a fix for deeper concerns.

## A common worry: will the hair grow back thicker?

This is the most common question, and the answer is no. Fine facial hair removed at the surface grows back the same way it was before. Dermaplaning does not change the hair or the follicle.
