Skin Script Rx is a botanically-based, professional skincare line used in every facial at this Buckeye studio. You'll find Skin Script Rx used in-treatment and available to take home, so your routine can continue past the appointment.

## What a visit looks like

Heaven Sent Beauty is a one-client-at-a-time studio, meaning there's no overlap, no rushing, and no stopwatch running on your treatment. A typical facial visit includes:

- A skin-first consult to talk through concerns and goals
- A customized facial using Skin Script Rx products, built around your skin rather than a template
- Finishing care and simple guidance for continuing your routine at home

This slower pace is intentional. The goal isn't just a cosmetic result on paper, it's leaving the studio feeling cared for and a little more like yourself.
