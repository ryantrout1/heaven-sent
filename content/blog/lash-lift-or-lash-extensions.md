# Lash Lift or Lash Extensions: Which Should You Choose?

Both make your eyes look more awake, but they work in very different ways. Here is a simple way to decide.

## The short version

- A **lash lift** works with the lashes you already have. It adds a natural curl and lift, so your lashes look bolder without mascara.
- **Lash extensions** add new lashes to your own, for more fullness, length and drama.

Neither is better. It comes down to the look you want and how much upkeep suits your life.

## Lash lift: your own lashes, lifted

A lash lift gives your natural lashes a curl that lifts them up and away from the eye. It is a good fit if you like a natural look, prefer to skip mascara, or want something low-fuss.

Many people pair a lift with a **lash tint**, which darkens the lashes for more definition and depth. Heaven Sent Beauty offers both.

## Lash extensions: added fullness

Extensions are added to your natural lashes one by one. There are three styles to choose from:

- **Classic.** Elegant, natural-looking fullness.
- **Hybrid.** A blend of classic and volume lashes for a textured look.
- **Volume.** Dramatic, fluffy lashes with maximum volume.

Extensions need fills to stay full. A fill refreshes and maintains your set, and each style has its own fill: classic, hybrid or volume.

## Which one is right for you?

A lash lift may suit you if you:

- Like a natural, everyday look
- Want to reduce how much mascara you wear
- Prefer less upkeep

Extensions may suit you if you:

- Want noticeably fuller or longer lashes
- Like a more dramatic look
- Are happy to come back for regular fills
