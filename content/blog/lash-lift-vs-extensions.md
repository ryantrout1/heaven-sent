# Lash Lift vs Lash Extensions: What's the Difference?

If you have landed here wondering whether a lash lift or lash extensions are the right move, you are not alone. It is one of the questions I get asked most in the studio, and the honest answer is: it depends on your natural lashes, your lifestyle, and the look you are after. Here is how I walk clients through it.

## What a Lash Lift Does

A lash lift works with the lashes you already have. It adds a natural curl and lift to your own lash line, so you wake up looking a little more awake without reaching for the mascara wand. There is no added length or extra fullness, just your lashes shown off at their best.

This is a lovely option if you like the idea of low-maintenance mornings but want to keep things simple and all your own. A Lash Tint can be added at the same visit to deepen the color and add a bit more definition.

## What Lash Extensions Do

Extensions add new lashes to your natural ones, which means more length, more fullness, and more drama if that is what you are after. I offer three styles:

- Classic Extensions, for elegant, natural-looking fullness
- Hybrid Extensions, a blend of classic and volume for extra texture
- Volume Extensions, for a dramatic, fluffy, full look

Because extensions grow out along with your natural lashes, they need regular fills to stay full. Each style has its own fill service so your set can be refreshed and maintained between full appointments.

## How to Choose Between Them

Think about three things: how full you want your lashes to look, how much upkeep you want to commit to, and how your natural lashes grow. A lift is a one-and-done kind of treatment that leans into what you already have. Extensions take more maintenance with fills, but they offer more transformation in length and volume.

There is no wrong choice here. When you book, tell me the look you are going for and how your mornings usually go, and I can help you decide what fits best.

## What the Appointment Feels Like

Whatever you choose, every lash appointment starts with a quiet conversation about your natural lashes and your goals. I see one client at a time, so there is never any rushing, and every set or lift is shaped around your own lash pattern rather than a one-size-fits-all template.
