# Brow Lamination Aftercare: How to Make Your Shape Last

Brow lamination sets your brow hairs into a smooth, lifted shape. How you care for them in the days after your appointment makes a real difference to how long that shape holds. New to the treatment? Start with [What Is Brow Lamination?](/blog/what-is-brow-lamination), then come back here for the aftercare.

## The first 24 to 48 hours

The days right after your appointment matter most for how long your lamination lasts and how smooth the shape stays. A few gentle habits go a long way:

- Keep brows dry for the first 24 hours, avoiding steam, sweat, and swimming
- Avoid touching, brushing, or picking at the hairs while they're still settling
- Skip makeup or brow products directly on the area for the first day
- Sleep in a way that avoids pressing your face into the pillow if possible

These small steps help the shape hold and keep the hairs looking soft rather than disrupted.

## Caring for your brows in the weeks after

Once the initial settling period passes, your brows will still benefit from a little extra care to keep them looking fresh:

- Brush brows gently into place each morning with a spoolie
- Avoid harsh exfoliants or retinol products directly on the brow area
- Use a light, nourishing oil or serum if your skin tends to feel dry
- Be gentle when cleansing your face, especially around the brow line

Brow lamination is a cosmetic treatment, so results soften naturally over time as new hairs grow in.

## How long it lasts, and when to come back

The shape holds for weeks, not forever. As your brows grow, the hairs slowly return to their natural direction. Many people find that spacing out their appointments works well once they get a feel for how their own brows hold the shape. If you would like a little more depth between visits, a brow tint can be added at your next appointment.
