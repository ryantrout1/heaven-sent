# What to Expect at Your First Facial

A first facial can feel like a mystery. You are not sure what to say, what to wear, or what will happen once you lie down. Here is what a first visit at Heaven Sent Beauty is like, step by step.

## Before you come

Hours are by appointment. You can book online or call 623-215-6084 to find a time.

You do not need to prepare much. It helps to think about:

- What you want from your skin, such as hydration, clearer skin or a glow
- The products you use now
- Anything your skin is sensitive to

## Step 1: the consultation

Every visit begins with a quiet conversation. You and Jayslyn Tramp, a licensed esthetician, talk through your skin and your goals before anything starts. This is the time to ask questions. The treatment is built around what you say, never around a template.

## Step 2: the treatment

Next comes a facial tailored to you, using professional, botanically-based Skin Script Rx products. Jayslyn sees one client at a time, with no rushing and no stopwatch. The room is yours to pause, breathe and relax.

How long you are there depends on the facial. A Customized Facial is 60 minutes, a Men's Facial is 45 minutes, and an Express Facial is 35 minutes.

## Step 3: afterward

You will leave with a simple way to carry your results home, and an open door whenever you are ready to return.

## Which facial should you book?

The Services page lists every facial, from hydrating and acne to anti-aging, dermaplaning and more. If you are not sure, a **Customized Facial** is a good place to start. It is tailored to your skin's unique needs, so you do not have to choose a category in advance.

Heaven Sent Beauty is at 111 Monroe Ave STE 101, Buckeye, AZ 85326. Hours are by appointment. You can see everything we offer on the [Services page](/services).
